# Underground Villages

A [Fabric](https://fabricmc.net/) mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1** that brings civilization to the caves:
underground villages carved into the rock, each with its own culture — and taverns where even
creepers learn some manners.

## Features

### 🏘️ Four underground village variants

| Village | Where | What you'll find |
|---|---|---|
| **Mining village** | Deep under most overworld biomes | Rail lines zigzagging between lantern-lit houses, minecarts, ore-flecked cavern walls, and **Miner** villagers |
| **Mushroom village** | Lush caves & mushroom fields | Houses built from giant mushroom blocks on mycelium paths, lit by shroomlight, home to **mushroom villagers** with caps growing on them |
| **Zombie village** | Dripstone caves, hills & snowy taiga | A dark, decayed, cobwebbed ruin inhabited entirely by zombie villagers |
| **Illager village** | The deep dark | Cobbled-deepslate watchtower and houses lit by soul lanterns, patrolled by vindicators and pillagers |

### ⛏️ Miner villagers & the Mining Hat
- New **Miner** profession with a **Prospecting Table** workstation.
- Miners trade **ore for ore** (copper ⇄ iron ⇄ gold), buy coal/lapis/redstone for emeralds — which
  is where villagers get their emeralds from — and sell rails, minecarts, torches and diamonds.
- At level 3 they sell the **Mining Hat**: a helmet that *actually emits light* while worn
  (the mod maintains invisible light blocks around your head as you move — works underwater too).

### 🍺 Taverns
Some villages contain a two-story **tavern**: jukebox, brewing stands, a bar, and bedrooms upstairs
hosting villagers, a witch, a wandering trader, a mercenary and a mysterious illager — all peaceful.

- The **Tavern Hearth** pacifies every hostile mob that enters the tavern — permanently. Bring your
  name-tagged creeper; it can finally retire.
- **Pacified witches trade potions** (each witch has her own potion menu).
- The **Tavern Keeper** villager sells things you can't get anywhere else: music discs, food, and
  **pre-tamed pets** — use the Wolf Whistle, Cat Bell or Parrot Cracker to summon a wolf, cat or
  parrot that is already yours.

### ⚔️ The Mercenary
Pay **30 emeralds** and the mercenary fights for you like an iron golem — following you and
attacking hostile mobs for **3 in-game days**, then heading home to the tavern. Re-hirable.

### 💀 The Collector
An illager at the tavern pays a fortune for trophies: **48 emeralds for a Nether Star**, **64 for a
Dragon Head**. But every deal funds the illagers — the **next raid** will arrive stronger, faster,
tougher, and wielding **enchanted weapons**. Trade carefully.

### 🧟 Igloo-style teaching structures
One house in the illager village hides a basement with a **wither summoning frame** — soul sand in
a T with one wither skeleton skull already placed (two, if you're lucky), showing how the wither is
built the same way the igloo basement teaches zombie-villager curing.

## Building

Requires Java 21.

```bash
./gradlew build
```

The mod jar lands in `build/libs/`. Requires the [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) ≥ 0.16
and [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) for Minecraft 1.21.1.

## Development notes

- Structure templates (`src/main/resources/data/undergroundvillages/structure/*.nbt`) are
  **generated** by `scripts/gen_structures.py` and validated by `scripts/validate_structures.py`
  (jigsaw wiring, palette, biome tags, entity ids). Run the generator after editing it; CI checks
  that committed templates match the generator output.
- Textures are generated by `scripts/gen_textures.py` (some derived from vanilla textures).
- Design spec: [`docs/SPEC.md`](docs/SPEC.md).
